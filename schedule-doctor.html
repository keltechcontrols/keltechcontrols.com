A Virtual House Call For Your P6 Schedule

Expert schedule review — with findings you can act on.

Many turnaround schedules look solid at a glance but contain logic breaks, hidden float risk, manpower loading issues, or inspection integration gaps that do not show up until execution is underway.

Schedule Doctor is a structured, independent review of your Primavera P6 schedule — delivered remotely, documented in writing, and backed by more than 30 years of owner-side refinery experience. The goal isn't just to identify what's wrong. It's to tell you what to do about it.

Two-Stage Engagement

Assessment first. Remediation if needed.

Stage 1 — Schedule Assessment

The core deliverable. Send a Primavera P6 XER export and every finding is documented — not just flagged. Each issue is identified, explained, and accompanied by a specific recommendation for correction. Findings are delivered as a written report and reviewed together by screen share so your team understands every item.

Most teams can begin addressing findings immediately after the review session.

Stage 2 — Remediation Support (Optional)

For teams that want the corrections made directly in the schedule, remediation support is available as a separate engagement. Scope and pricing are quoted after Stage 1 findings are delivered — so you know exactly what needs to be fixed before committing to additional work.

This keeps Stage 1 clean and gives your team full visibility into findings before deciding on next steps.
